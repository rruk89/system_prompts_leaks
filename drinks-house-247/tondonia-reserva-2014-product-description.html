Questions

How does the 2014 compare with the 2011?
Both are classic Tondonia Reservas from the same vineyard and the same old vats. The 2014 comes from a wetter year and shows a fresh, classic style, with blackcurrant, cherry and mocha. The 2011 has now sold out; the 2014 is the current release.
Why does López de Heredia ferment in such old oak vats?
The house still uses vats over 140 years old rather than modern stainless steel, part of a deliberate commitment to traditional Rioja winemaking that's become increasingly rare in the region.
Why does this wine take so long to release?
Reserva rules in Rioja require significant ageing, but López de Heredia goes well beyond the minimum, holding its wines for years in American oak barrels and then in bottle before they're sold.
What's distinctive about the Tondonia vineyard?
It's 247 acres of alluvial clay with high limestone content on the right bank of the River Ebro, acquired by the estate's founder in 1913 — one of the original single vineyards recognised in Rioja.
