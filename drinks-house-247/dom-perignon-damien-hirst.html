The Collaboration

Dom Pérignon has made vintage-only Champagne since 1936, capturing a single year in every cuvée. For its 2026 limited edition, the house invited British artist Damien Hirst to reinterpret its famous shield label. Hirst saw in its outline the shape of a butterfly wing, the motif at the heart of his Kaleidoscope works, and repeated it into a symmetrical, stained-glass pattern across the bottle and gift box. A black neck collar carries the words "Limited Edition" and Hirst's signature.

The result is a Champagne that is as much a collectible art object as a celebration bottle. It also joins a long line of Dom Pérignon artist editions, several of which you'll find below.

Dom Pérignon x Damien Hirst: FAQs

Can I get Dom Pérignon x Damien Hirst delivered in London today?

Yes. Both the Vintage 2018 and Rosé 2010 editions are in stock for same-day delivery across London in 30–45 minutes, 24 hours a day. We also deliver next day across the UK mainland.

What is the difference between the two Hirst editions?

The Vintage 2018, in deep blue and violet, is radiant and fruit-led with orchard, tropical and apricot notes. The Rosé 2010, in magenta and pink, is intense, precise and silky. Both come in Hirst's Kaleidoscope gift box.

Is the Damien Hirst Dom Pérignon a limited edition?

Yes. It is Dom Pérignon's limited edition for 2026, and each bottle carries a "Limited Edition" neck collar with Damien Hirst's signature.

Can I send it as a gift with caviar?

Yes, there is a hamper for each edition. The Hirst Prestige Hamper pairs the Vintage 2018 with 30g of Royal Oscietra caviar and chocolate almonds. The Kaleidoscope Rosé Hamper pairs the Rosé 2010 with 30g of Royal Oscietra caviar and Charbonnel et Walker pink Marc de Champagne truffles. Both include a mother-of-pearl spoon, crème fraîche, mini blinis and your personalised message.
