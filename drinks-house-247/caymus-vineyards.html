About Caymus Vineyards

Caymus Vineyards was founded in Rutherford, in the heart of Napa Valley, in 1972 by Chuck Wagner with his parents, Lorna and Charlie Sr. The first release was just 240 cases. The family has made Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon every year since, sourcing fruit from across Napa's sub-appellations, from cooler Coombsville in the south to warmer Calistoga in the north, plus Rutherford and Oakville. Caymus is still 100% family-run, with the next generation of Wagners now involved in farming and winemaking.

Which Caymus vintages do you stock?

We currently stock Caymus Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon 1993, 1995, 2003 and the 50th-anniversary 2022. The older vintages are library bottles in limited quantities.

Is an old Caymus still good to drink?

Mature Napa Cabernet can drink well at 20 to 30 years old, but every old bottle is individual, since its condition depends on how it was stored. Expect a softer, more savoury wine than a current release.

Why is the 2022 Caymus special?

2022 is Caymus's 50th-anniversary vintage, and it scored 95 points from Natalie MacLean.
