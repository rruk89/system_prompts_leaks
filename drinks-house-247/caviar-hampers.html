A Guide to Caviar

Caviar is the salted roe of the sturgeon, and the species decides the size, colour and flavour of the pearls. These are the five types we stock. For more detail, read our guides to Beluga caviar and Oscietra caviar.

Baeri

Acipenser baerii · Siberian sturgeon

Medium, dark pearls with a creamy, nutty flavour. The most approachable caviar and the best place to start.

Prunier Baeri Tradition →

Oscietra

Acipenser gueldenstaedtii

Firm brown to golden pearls with a clean, nutty finish. The caviar in most of our hampers.

Croe Royal Oscietra →

Kaluga

Huso dauricus · "river beluga"

Large, shiny amber grains, buttery and hazelnut-rich. Beluga-sized pearls at a lower price.

Caviar House Pure Kaluga →

Beluga

Huso huso

The largest pearls, pearl-grey, delicate and creamy with a long finish. The sturgeon can take up to 20 years to mature.

Croe Imperial Beluga →

Almas

Albino sturgeon · "white caviar"

Rare, pale golden-white pearls, very light and clean. The rarest caviar we sell.

Caviar House Almas →

How to Serve Caviar

0–4°CKeep the tin in the coldest part of the fridge until you serve
PearlUse a mother-of-pearl spoon: silver and steel can taint the flavour
15–30gPer person as a starter; a 30g tin serves one to two
BrutDry Champagne or ice-cold vodka. Blanc de Blancs is the classic match

Serve on warm blinis with a little crème fraîche, or simply from the back of the hand. Once opened, eat within a few days. All our hampers include the spoon, blinis and crème fraîche. More tips in our guide on how to serve caviar.

Caviar Hampers: FAQs

Can I get a caviar hamper delivered in London today?

Yes. Every caviar hamper and tin on this page can be delivered across Greater London in 30–45 minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. We also deliver next day across the UK mainland.

What is in a caviar and Champagne hamper?

Each hamper pairs a bottle, such as Dom Pérignon, Cristal or Ruinart, with a tin of caviar (usually 30g of Royal Oscietra) and a full caviar service: a mother-of-pearl spoon, mini blinis and crème fraîche. Most also include fine chocolate and a personalised gift message.

Which Champagne goes best with caviar?

A dry, fresh Champagne. Blanc de Blancs, such as Ruinart Blanc de Blancs, is the classic match, and vintage Champagnes like Dom Pérignon and Cristal have the depth to stand up to Oscietra and Beluga. Ice-cold vodka is the other traditional partner.

How much caviar do I need per person?

Allow 15–30g per person as a starter. A 30g tin serves one to two people, and the 50g tins in the Cristal Prestige and Dom Pérignon P2 2003 hampers are enough for two to three to share.

What is the difference between Oscietra, Beluga and Baeri caviar?

They come from different sturgeon. Baeri (Siberian sturgeon) has medium, dark, creamy pearls and is the most affordable. Oscietra has firm brown to golden pearls with a nutty finish. Beluga (Huso huso) has the largest, most delicate pearl-grey pearls and is the most prized.

What is the best caviar hamper for a wedding or anniversary?

The Dom Pérignon & Caviar Hamper is our most-gifted choice for weddings, and the Prestige Pearl with Ruinart Blanc de Blancs is a close second.

Do you have an alcohol-free caviar hamper?

Yes. The Caviar & Champagne Hamper (Alcohol-Free) pairs French Bloom alcohol-free sparkling with 30g of Royal Oscietra for £180, and the Eid Hamper adds dates and nuts.

How should I store caviar?

Keep the unopened tin in the coldest part of the fridge, between 0 and 4°C, and don't freeze it. Once opened, eat it within a few days.
